The Problem

Built for men.
Used by everyone.

01

Generic programming ignores female physiology

Every major training app assumes a 24-hour hormonal cycle. Women operate on a 28-day cycle with four distinct phases — each one changing strength, recovery, and energy capacity.

02

You're fighting your own biology every week

Pushing max effort in follicular is different from pushing the same load in luteal. Ignoring that gap leads to overtraining, stalled progress, and unnecessary fatigue.

03

Period trackers don't talk to your training

Flo and Clue track symptoms. They don't adapt your program. CYCLEX does the one thing they don't: connects your cycle data to your actual workout plan.
